## Prior work and distinction

Local secret redaction is an existing product category. Klipshot markets local redaction for developers. The proposed contribution here is a narrow temporal review workflow, conservative handling of processing failure, explicit brief-exposure evaluation and measured Snapdragon integration. No global novelty, patent clearance or superiority to untested competitors is claimed.
